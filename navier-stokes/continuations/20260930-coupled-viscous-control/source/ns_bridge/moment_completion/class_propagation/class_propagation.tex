\section{Finite propagation of the actual coupled source classes}
\label{mccp:section}

This section records the finite class statement that follows from the
actual coupled operation. It is a statement about a fixed source band,
a fixed finite set of labels, and the physical viscosity
$\nu_{\rm NS}>0$. No parameter is normalized. In particular
$Q=2^{-\ell}$, $\varepsilon=Q^h$, $S_* = \ell^2$, and the original
edge weights, pulse envelope, phase maps, and source constants are kept.
The estimates below propagate a pre-existing locally finite source family
and a newly added finite family simultaneously.

\subsection{The source classes and their exact closure}

Fix a common auxiliary torus $H$ and one source rectangle. Put
\[
 Q=2^{-\ell},\qquad \varepsilon=Q^h,\qquad
 R=r/\sqrt Q,\quad Z=z/Q^D,\quad T=(1-t)/Q,
 \qquad A=\tfrac12+h,\quad D=\tfrac12-h,
\]
where $0<h<1/100$. For $X=QR^2/(2q)$, retain
\[
 \zeta(X)=\exp\!\left(-\frac{a_a}{\log^2(X/X_a)}
       -\frac{a_b}{\log^2(X_b/X)}\right),\qquad
 \delta(X)=\min\{1,\log(X/X_a),\log(X_b/X)\},
\]
with the original positive $a_a,a_b$ and the original interval
$X_a<X<X_b$. The pulse variable is the source variable
$0\leq v\leq L_s$, and ${\cal P}_\gamma(v)$ denotes the exact positive
exponential pulse envelope from the source equation (7.12), including
its Gaussian factors and its fixed source parameters.

For a source order $\alpha\in\mathbb R$ define the following classes.
A mean coefficient $f\in M^\alpha$ is independent of the angular
variable, descends to $H$, is smoothly zero outside the active shell,
and obeys, for each finite multi-index $I$ in $(R,Z,T,H)$,
\begin{equation}\label{mccp:Mclass}
 |\partial^I f|\le C_I\varepsilon^\alpha S_*^{b_I}
                  \zeta\,\delta^{-d_I}.
\end{equation}
A moment coefficient $F\in S^\alpha$ obeys
\begin{equation}\label{mccp:Sclass}
 |\partial_{Z,T,H}^I F|\le C_I\varepsilon^\alpha S_*^{b_I}.
\end{equation}
A nonzero labelled amplitude $a_{\gamma,m}\in W^\alpha$ is independent
of the angular variable, has the source slow, transverse, and shell
support, compatible representatives on all integer covers, and obeys
\begin{equation}\label{mccp:Wclass}
 |\partial^I a_{\gamma,m}|
 \le C_I\varepsilon^\alpha S_*^{b_I}
       \sqrt\zeta\,\delta^{-d_I}{\cal P}_\gamma(v).
\end{equation}
The constants in these three displays may depend on the fixed finite
stage and $I$, but are uniform over the finitely many retained labels and
representatives. Equal label--harmonic terms are summed before applying
these bounds.

The differential maps used below are exact source maps, with the phase
factor removed:
\begin{equation}\label{mccp:derivative_maps}
 \partial_{R,Z,T}:C^\alpha\to C^\alpha,\quad
 D_r:C^\alpha\to C^{\alpha-\kappa_s},\quad
 D_z=\varepsilon\partial_Z:C^\alpha\to C^{\alpha+1},
\end{equation}
\[
 c_{i_0}N_{i_0}:C^\alpha\to C^\alpha,qquad
 -\varepsilon\partial_T:C^\alpha\to C^{\alpha+1},
 \qquad C=M,W,
\]
where $\kappa_s=10^{-5}$ and $c_{i_0},N_{i_0}$ are the original source
coefficient and torus transport operator. Products satisfy
\begin{equation}\label{mccp:product_closure}
 M^\alpha M^\beta\subset M^{\alpha+\beta},\qquad
 M^\alpha W^\beta\subset W^{\alpha+\beta}.
\end{equation}
For equal labels, products of two $W$ coefficients belong to
$M^{\alpha+\beta}$ in the zero output harmonic and to
$W^{\alpha+\beta}$ in every other harmonic. Distinct disjointly
supported labels have zero product. These inclusions follow by multiplying
(\ref{mccp:Mclass})--(\ref{mccp:Wclass}): the factors multiply to
$\varepsilon^{\alpha+\beta}S_*^{b_I+b_J}$, while
$(\sqrt\zeta)^2=\zeta$ and ${\cal P}_\gamma$ is retained in every
nonzero output. Leibniz' rule and the finite multinomial sum prove the
same statement for every derivative $I$. Thus no product is discarded as
``bookkeeping''.

The prescribed source phases retain the exact arithmetic condition
\begin{equation}\label{mccp:phase_arithmetic}
 k_\gamma p_\gamma\in\mathbb Z\setminus\{0\},\qquad
 k_\gamma=\lceil\varepsilon^{-1/2}\rceil .
\end{equation}
The finite harmonic set is independent of the band at a fixed stage, and
all equal label--harmonic terms are grouped before testing.

\subsection{Old family plus finite actual increment}

Let $R_{\rm old}$ be the full residual of the entering coupled state,
including every old wave, old mean, old pressure, phase derivative, and
viscous term. Assume only the attributed source bounds for its locally
finite family: on each compact source support and for each derivative
$I$, its mean coefficients, moment coefficients, and nonzero harmonic
coefficients satisfy (\ref{mccp:Mclass})--(\ref{mccp:Wclass}). This is a
local finiteness assertion; it does not set any old coefficient equal to
zero.

Let $\Delta U=(a_r,a_\theta,a_z)$ be the actual finite increment produced
by the five-row operation, with
\[
 a_\theta=q^A{\cal P}V_P+q^{A-1}{\cal J}_zV_{J_z},
 \qquad a_z=q^{A-3/2}{\cal J}_\theta G_{J_\theta},
\]
\[
 \Psi=q^{A-1}{\cal J}_\theta H_\theta,
 \qquad a_r=-\partial_z\Psi.
\]
Here $V_P,V_{J_z},G_{J_\theta},H_\theta$ are the exact inverse
Vandermonde profiles and compact combined primitive of the five-row
operation; their coefficients contain the original $a(\eta)$,
$\lambda$, $d_b$, moments $\mu_p$, and inverse matrices $B_\theta,B_z$.
The targets are full current targets
\[
 {\cal P}=P_{\rm old}+\Delta P,\quad
 {\cal J}_\theta=(J_\theta)_{\rm old}+\Delta J_\theta,\quad
 {\cal J}_z=(J_z)_{\rm old}+\Delta J_z,
\]
not merely the increments. The exact five rows are
\begin{equation}\label{mccp:five_rows}
\begin{gathered}
 \int r^2a_\theta\,dr=0,\qquad \int ra_z\,dr=0,\qquad
 \int \frac{2V}{r}a_\theta\,dr=-{\cal P},\\
 \int r^2(Ga_\theta+Va_z)\,dr=-{\cal J}_\theta,\\
 \int(2rGa_z-rVa_\theta)\,dr=-{\cal J}_z.
\end{gathered}
\end{equation}
The inverse matrices prove (\ref{mccp:five_rows}) at the original $q$,
without changing signs or setting $a(\eta)$ to one. In particular the
combined primitive has zero tail because the exact first axial moment of
$G_{J_\theta}$ is zero; an individual primitive need not be compact.

On the fixed compact support of the finite increment, all derivatives of
$q$, $\eta$, and the profiles are finite by the exact dictionaries
\[
 q_z=\frac{2\eta q^A}{B},\quad q_t=-B^{-1},\quad
 \eta_z=\frac{q^{-D}(1-\eta^2)}B,\quad
 \eta_t=\frac{D\eta q^{-1}}B,
 \quad B=1-2h\eta^2>0,
\]
\[
 \partial_r(q^\sigma F)=q^{\sigma-1/2}F_x,\quad
 \partial_z(q^\sigma F)=q^{\sigma-D}{\cal Z}_\sigma F,\quad
 \partial_t(q^\sigma F)=q^{\sigma-1}{\cal T}_\sigma F.
\]
Repeated product and chain rules therefore produce explicit finite
majorants for every $\partial_r^k\partial_z^m\partial_t^n a_i$.
For $I=(m,n)$ define, using the full target derivative arrays,
\begin{equation}\label{mccp:target_majorants}
\begin{aligned}
 \mathsf P_I&=|\partial^IP_{\rm old}|+{\cal B}_{-1,I}
                         +\tfrac12{\cal B}_{0,(m+1,n)},\\
 \mathsf J_{\theta,I}&=|\partial^I(J_\theta)_{\rm old}|
                         +\tfrac12{\cal B}_{2,I},\\
 \mathsf J_{z,I}&=|\partial^I(J_z)_{\rm old}|+{\cal B}_{1,I}
                         +\tfrac14{\cal B}_{2,(m+1,n)},
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
where ${\cal B}_{e,I}$ is the exact weighted old-wave, cross-wave, and
self-wave derivative sum. The triangle inequality gives
$|\partial^I{\cal P}|\le\mathsf P_I$ and the analogous two bounds for
${\cal J}_\theta,{\cal J}_z$. Consequently the increment derivative
arrays are finite explicit sums of the target arrays times the profile
recursions. This proves finite smoothness of the added family, including
all mixed derivatives and every factor $q^{-D}$ generated by $a_r$.

\subsection{Pressure defect and pressure derivative class}

Let $\delta g_r$ be the exact negative radial residual change before the
new pressure, including the old velocity, the complete increment, all
wave cross terms, and $\nu_{\rm NS}(\Delta_0-r^{-2})a_r$. The full vector
force identity is
\begin{equation}\label{mccp:full_force_identity}
 \delta R=\partial_t\Delta U+(U\cdot\nabla)\Delta U
 +(\Delta U\cdot\nabla)U+(\Delta U\cdot\nabla)\Delta U
 -\nu_{\rm NS}\Delta\Delta U+\nabla\delta p,
\end{equation}
where $U$ is the entering full velocity and $\delta p$ is the actual
reconstructed pressure below. In cylindrical components the self terms
are $a_ra_{r,r}+a_za_{r,z}-a_\theta^2/r$,
$a_ra_{\theta,r}+a_za_{\theta,z}+a_ra_\theta/r$, and
$a_ra_{z,r}+a_za_{z,z}$; the viscosity is
$\nu_{\rm NS}((\Delta_0-r^{-2})a_r,
(\Delta_0-r^{-2})a_\theta,\Delta_0a_z)$.
Thus every inverse-radius connection term is part of $\delta g_r$.

Fix a compact physical interval $[R_-,R_+]\Subset(0,\infty)$ containing
all increment and pressure-bump supports, with $L_R=R_+-R_-$. Let
$\mathsf G_{k,m,n}$ be the supremum over that interval and all original
auxiliary phases of the pressure-free radial force cost obtained from
(\ref{mccp:full_force_identity}), deleting $\partial_r\delta p$ but
retaining all old-wave derivatives, inverse radii, and $\nu_{\rm NS}$. Then
\begin{equation}\label{mccp:pressure_free}
 |\partial_r^k\partial_z^m\partial_t^n(\delta g_r)_{\rm phys}|
 \le\mathsf G_{k,m,n},\qquad
 |\partial_z^m\partial_t^n\delta P_{\rm step}|
 \le L_R\mathsf G_{0,m,n}=:\mathsf D_{m,n}.
\end{equation}
The second inequality follows by differentiating the compact radial
integral under the original Haar average. It does not use a sign for any
scale or amplitude.

The physical bump is $\rho=q^{-1/2}\widehat\rho(r/\sqrt q)$, with its
exact recursion giving
\[
 |\partial_r^k\partial_z^m\partial_t^n\rho|
 \le \mathsf H_{k,m,n}:=
 q^{-(1+k)/2-mD-n}\sup|\rho_{k,m,n}(r/\sqrt q,\eta)|.
\]
For $f=(\delta g_r)_{\rm phys}-\rho\,\delta P_{\rm step}$ set
\begin{equation}\label{mccp:pressure_source_array}
 \mathsf E_{k,m,n}=\mathsf G_{k,m,n}+
 \sum_{i=0}^m\sum_{j=0}^n\binom{m}{i}\binom{n}{j}
       \mathsf H_{k,m-i,n-j}\mathsf D_{i,j}.
\end{equation}
The exact physical phase evaluation of the shifted pressure primitive is
\[
 \delta p(r,z,t)=\int_0^r f_{\rm phys}(s,z,t)\,ds
 -\chi_m(r,z,t)\int_0^\infty f_{\rm phys}(s,z,t)\,ds.
\]
The cutoff derivative array is
$\mathsf X_{k,i,j}=|\partial_r^k\partial_z^i\partial_t^j\chi_m|$. Therefore
\begin{equation}\label{mccp:pressure_array}
 \mathsf\Pi_{k,m,n}=\begin{cases}L_R\mathsf E_{0,m,n}&k=0,\\
 \mathsf E_{k-1,m,n}&k\ge1,\end{cases}
 +L_R\sum_{i,j}\binom{m}{i}\binom{n}{j}
       \mathsf X_{k,i,j}\mathsf E_{0,m-i,n-j}
\end{equation}
(with the sum ranges $0\le i\le m$, $0\le j\le n$). This proves
$|\partial_r^k\partial_z^m\partial_t^n\delta p|\le\mathsf\Pi_{k,m,n}$.
The auxiliary average of the shifted tail is zero, but the tail itself and
its cutoff derivative remain in (\ref{mccp:pressure_array}). Thus this is a
pressure derivative class bound for the actual reconstructed pressure,
not for its auxiliary average.

\subsection{Full force costs and class propagation}

For a scalar derivative multi-index
$\alpha=(k,\ell,m,n)$ let $\star$ denote the exact Leibniz convolution
\[
 (X\star Y)_\alpha=\sum_{\beta\le\alpha}\binom{\alpha}{\beta}
 X_\beta Y_{\alpha-\beta},\qquad
 (S_\delta X)_\alpha=X_{\alpha+\delta}.
\]
Let $\mathsf A_i$ majorize all component derivatives of $a_i$, and
$\mathsf U_i=|\partial^\alpha U_i|$. Let $\mathsf R_j$ be the exact
absolute derivative array of $r^{-j}$, namely
$(\mathsf R_j)_\alpha=(j)_k r^{-j-k}$ when $\ell=m=n=0$ and zero otherwise.
Define
\begin{equation}\label{mccp:transport_cost}
\begin{split}
 \mathsf T_i={}&S_{e_t}\mathsf A_i+\mathsf U_r\star S_{e_r}\mathsf A_i
 +\mathsf U_z\star S_{e_z}\mathsf A_i
 +\mathsf A_r\star S_{e_r}\mathsf U_i
 +\mathsf A_z\star S_{e_z}\mathsf U_i\\
 &+\mathsf R_1\star\mathsf A_\theta\star S_{e_\theta}\mathsf U_i
 +\mathsf A_r\star S_{e_r}\mathsf A_i
 +\mathsf A_z\star S_{e_z}\mathsf A_i,
\end{split}
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}\label{mccp:force_costs}
\begin{aligned}
 \mathsf F_r={}&\mathsf T_r+2\mathsf R_1\star\mathsf U_\theta\star\mathsf A_\theta
 +\mathsf R_1\star\mathsf A_\theta\star\mathsf A_\theta\\
 &+\nu_{\rm NS}(S_{2e_r}\mathsf A_r+\mathsf R_1\star S_{e_r}\mathsf A_r
 +S_{2e_z}\mathsf A_r+\mathsf R_2\star\mathsf A_r)+S_{e_r}\mathsf\Pi,\\
 \mathsf F_\theta={}&\mathsf T_\theta+\mathsf R_1\star(\mathsf U_r\star\mathsf A_\theta
 +\mathsf A_r\star\mathsf U_\theta+\mathsf A_r\star\mathsf A_\theta)\\
 &+\nu_{\rm NS}(S_{2e_r}\mathsf A_\theta+\mathsf R_1\star S_{e_r}\mathsf A_\theta
 +S_{2e_z}\mathsf A_\theta+\mathsf R_2\star\mathsf A_\theta)
 +\mathsf R_1\star S_{e_\theta}\mathsf\Pi,\\
 \mathsf F_z={}&\mathsf T_z+\nu_{\rm NS}(S_{2e_r}\mathsf A_z
 +\mathsf R_1\star S_{e_r}\mathsf A_z+S_{2e_z}\mathsf A_z)
 +S_{e_z}\mathsf\Pi.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
By the repeated Leibniz rule, (\ref{mccp:force_costs}) proves
\begin{equation}\label{mccp:force_bound}
 |\partial^\alpha\delta R_i|\le\mathsf F_{i,\alpha},
 \qquad i=r,\theta,z.
\end{equation}
The proof retains all angular derivatives of the old full velocity in
$\mathsf U$, all connection terms, the pressure tail, and physical
viscosity. It also retains the full quadratic increment; no fixed-
background conjugacy is used.

The source-class consequence is finite and exact. On the compact support
of the added increment, $\varepsilon$, $\zeta$, $\delta$, and each fixed
positive ${\cal P}_\gamma(v)$ have positive minima. Local finiteness of
the old family leaves only finitely many old labels there. For every
fixed derivative $I$ and every added label define the concrete ratio
\[
 K_{I,\gamma,m}=
 \max_{\operatorname{supp}(\Delta R)\cap\operatorname{supp}(\gamma,m)}
 \frac{|\partial^I(\Delta R)_{\gamma,m}|}
 {\varepsilon^\alpha S_*^{b_I}\sqrt\zeta\,\delta^{-d_I}{\cal P}_\gamma(v)},
\]
with the numerator and denominator evaluated using the original phase
representative; if the support is empty set $K_{I,\gamma,m}=0$.
Compactness and positivity prove $K_{I,\gamma,m}<\infty$. Extend the
added family by zero on unused labels. Then the old-plus-added residual
satisfies the source class inequalities on this finite stage, with the
old constants and the finite constants $K_{I,\gamma,m}$ both retained.
This is a finite compact-ratio conclusion only: it gives no uniform bound
when labels, bands, or stages increase, and it gives no infinite gain.

For the ordered source cycle, under the support, zero-mean, and phase
hypotheses of the corresponding source propositions, the four actual
operations have the following attributed class maps:
\[
 W^\alpha\xrightarrow{\rm pulse}W^\alpha\oplus W^{\alpha+1/2},\qquad
 M^\alpha_{\,\mathrm{aux\text{-}independent}}
       \xrightarrow{\rm stress}W^{\alpha-1/2},
\]
\[
 S^\alpha\xrightarrow{\rm temporal}M^\alpha,
 \qquad S^\alpha\xrightarrow{\rm five\ rows}M^\alpha\oplus M^{\alpha+1}.
\]
The full residual produced after each operation is recomputed from
(\ref{mccp:full_force_identity}), (\ref{mccp:pressure_array}), and the
exact nonlinear defect expansion; in particular the varying-bump term,
the pressure cutoff remainder, old-wave cross terms, and quadratic terms
are retained. The source's attributed finite-cycle budget is
\[
 w\in W^{1/2},\quad w-w_0^{\tan}\in W^{0.68},\quad
 (v,\gamma,p_m)\in M^{0.9},\quad \beta\in M^{1.9},
\]
with $B_j=1/2+\sigma_j$, $C_j^*=1+\sigma_j$, and
$\sigma_j=1/5+j/10$. Its claimed gain is
$B_{j+1}-B_j=C_{j+1}^*-C_j^*=1/10$ for the source's finite ordered
cycle. The present derivation proves the exact finite maps and costs on
the actual coupled state; it does not promote the attributed cycle to a
uniform infinite modified sequence.

Finally, replacing every source class by its sign-reversed profile leaves
(\ref{mccp:Mclass})--(\ref{mccp:Wclass}) unchanged, because the estimates
are absolute values. The sign of the actual positive or reflected swirl is
therefore carried by the coefficients and force, while the class bound
itself is not a positive-scale assertion. A reflection of one coordinate
flips the corresponding vorticity component and transforms the force by
$\rho O$; it does not identify $u$ with $-u$. This is the exact scope of
the negative-branch propagation.
