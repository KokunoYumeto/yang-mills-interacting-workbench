# D1: square-root grouping corrected in equation (10.20)

The LaTeX, PDF and source ZIP now place the whole product under the square root:

```tex
x_\tau=(\sqrt{2X_{\mathrm{in}}\tau},0,0),\qquad t=1-\tau.
```

Previously the transcription used `\sqrt2X_{\mathrm{in}}\tau`, placing only
the 2 under the radical. The affected occurrence is `NS-F-P124-005`, line 306
of `reconstruction/sections/pp121-126.tex`. This is a transcription error:
the official paper already prints the full radicand.
