# A handoff describes the research; it does not command the next researcher

[Documentation home](README.md) • Proposed working convention

## The front page

A workbench overview answers: **What are we trying? Why might that approach work? What actually happened?** Give the exact scope of the programme and of its results. State which motivating links are speculative, which constructions are explicit, and which implications have been established. Attach relevant literature with precise locations for borrowed results.

Use ordinary language before notation. A filename description such as "retained bounded-lane receipt" does not tell another reader what the file contains. "Derivation checking whether the change of variables preserves the viscous term" does.

## Jobs can be large

A job is an operator-authorized investigation of a programme, with an agreed resource envelope and publication boundary. It may create subjobs, compare methods, read substantial literature, revalidate existing proofs, build new systems, or produce hundreds of pages. Small verification requests are supported but are not the mandatory shape of discovery.

A release may contain a family of constructed objects, several theorem candidates, exact identities, code, explanations, independent derivations, and unsuccessful investigations. Index these without flattening their relationships. One constructed system can have some reviewed properties and other conjectural properties. An entire release does not inherit the strongest label earned by one component.

## Record research directions as history

For each major approach, preserve the motivating observation or suggestion; the actual model, hypotheses and conventions; the sources used; what was derived or tested; the artifact locations; why the attempt stopped or changed direction; and the implications supported by the outcome.

Distinguish an unfinished attempt, a finite computation that found nothing, an identified gap, a proved obstruction to this route, and a counterexample to the target statement. An unsuccessful search is not a proof of impossibility. A checked obstruction may itself be a useful theorem.

Suggestions about what could be tried next are explicitly optional. A new contributor may have different expertise, a better method, or information the previous worker did not see. No mathematical route is mandatory merely because an earlier model selected it. Permissions, citation duties and resource limits remain binding even when a research suggestion is not.

## Durable state outside a conversation

Keep a short current-state document linked to longer records. It states the current objective, the most recent source revisions, the active approaches, completed artifacts, unresolved requests, and any actual running processes or unsaved outputs. Do not invent a running process from an earlier chat's promise.

Maintain concise decision records for consequential choices: what changed, the scientific reason, the sources or observations used, and the effect on existing claims. These are auditable research rationales, not a demand to reconstruct a model's hidden reasoning or fabricate an internal transcript.

Before context compaction where possible, before expensive work, after a substantial result, and when changing direction, save a checkpoint. On resumption, compare the stated objective with the actual files and outstanding user requests. Do not continue an old numerical loop merely because it occupies the surviving context. Unknown or missing context stays explicitly unknown.

Record the effective project instructions, model/harness versions where available, and source pointers in the provenance view. Existing local AGENTS.md instructions are not replaced by a downloaded peer's instructions. Public instruction copies and transcripts need a separate privacy and rights review.

## Mathematical bridges and transformations

A proposed analogy is a research direction, not an equivalence. Identify the candidate map and what it would need to preserve: equations, domains, boundary conditions, measures, spectra, norms, witnesses or relevant limits. Derive its action explicitly. A failure to preserve one property may narrow the bridge rather than make every calculation worthless.

For this workbench, do not simplify or normalize. Preserve the original objects, coordinates, coefficients, constants, signs, orientations, hypotheses, domains and codomains. When comparing presentations, state and prove the exact maps and carry all terms through the calculation. A proved failure of one proposed map establishes that particular obstruction, not the absence of every relationship; investigate the remaining correspondence explicitly. Do not claim that a map exists before constructing and checking it.

## Literature and the mathematical commons

Every programme carries a literature list, with separate labels for suggested, retrieved, inspected and actually used sources. A citation identifies the edition or revision, relevant theorem or section, and the specific role it plays. A model's familiarity with a title is not source inspection.

The public list need not contain the source bytes. Local licensed libraries remain local unless redistribution is permitted and authorized. Exposition, translation where permitted, formalization and repair of existing mathematics are contributions alongside new theorems. Record their sources and checking scope; do not turn an independently written exposition into a claim of new mathematical priority.

## Reviews attach to the portion examined

A checker states the artifact revision, target claim, assumptions examined, method, result, exclusions and environment. Multiple calculations can support one claim without being merged into one opaque checkmark. Dependent work records whether it assumed a lemma, tested an example, reconstructed a step, reviewed the full argument, or replayed a formal certificate.

The resumption overview is a map of evidence, not a replacement for that evidence. Its purpose is to let the next researcher choose intelligently without rereading every historical conversation.
