# S6 reconstruction: complete public checkpoint of 6 September 2026

Start with [Exact maps and analytic certificates: key advances](26_s6_key_advances_frozen_2026-09-06.pdf), the unchanged five-page reader prepared on 6 September. Its [complete TeX source](27_s6_key_advances_frozen_2026-09-06.tex) is supplied separately.

The reader describes the anticanonical ring and intrinsic fibration, the original period-parameter deformation, explicit finite fillings and attachment maps, the marked Niemeier–Leech cubic arithmetic, and the literal common lattice in the original triality space. Its formulas, qualifications and references to complete proofs remain unchanged. Printed proof locations refer to the frozen 138-page higher-rung paper and 782-page cumulative workbench inside this checkpoint.

The [complete public project archive](28_s6_complete_public_project_frozen_2026-09-06.zip) preserves every file and relative path of the prepared 6 September public edition: 1,079 files and 221,140,515 uncompressed bytes. It includes the full project TeX and bibliography, standalone papers and supplied PDFs, exact programs and recorded results, the complete compressed calculation dataset, historical mathematical material, and the 28 earlier published version 1.2 artifacts unchanged under `original_published/v1.2/`. Open `project/workbench.pdf` for the cumulative reader and `project/s6_higher_rung_24d_preprint.pdf` for the triality and lattice continuation. The source counterparts have the same stems and the `.tex` extension.

The archive root contains `README.md` and `BUILD.md`. Its `manifests/FILE_MANIFEST.json` records every other archive member by relative path, byte length and SHA-256. `SOURCE_ACCOUNTING.json`, `TRANSFORM_MANIFEST.json` and `HISTORICAL_LOCATOR_MAP.md` explain the public edition's source dispositions, documented public derivatives and historical references. Previously prepared public derivatives preserve mathematical content while removing private operational material. Newly collected private logs, credentials and local review receipts are not archive inputs.

This package was assembled on 9 September from the exact frozen 6 September public tree. Packaging did not update the mathematics, rebuild the PDFs, or rerun the mathematical calculations. The outer [package manifest](30_S6_FROZEN_PROJECT_PACKAGE_MANIFEST_2026-09-09.json) identifies the archive, direct readers and this guide. Archive membership, file hashes and ZIP round-trip integrity were checked independently of the earlier mathematical and visual work. Historical status records retain their original dates; later research is outside this frozen checkpoint.
