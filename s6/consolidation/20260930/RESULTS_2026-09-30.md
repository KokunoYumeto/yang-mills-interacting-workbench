tangency $m=\beta$ exactly when $0<p<\tfrac12$. On the full positive

$$
m^{-p}\,{}_2F_1\!\left(\frac p2,\frac{p+1}{2};1;
\frac{\beta^2}{m^2}\right).

$$
\frac{(-1)^{p-1}}{(p-1)!}
\partial_m^{p-1}(m^2-\beta^2)^{-1/2}.
$$
