# Status, 5 October 2026: complex structures on S6 and the Campana–Demailly–Peternell theorem

## Summary

- The construction circulated by Levent Alpöge and produced with Claude [AC26] gives complex structures on $S^6$. Philip Engel has posted an independent, self-contained proof on arXiv [E26].
- The construction gives a one-parameter family of complex structures on $S^6$. Every member fibres holomorphically over $\mathbb P^1$, so every member has algebraic dimension at least $1$. [AC26] and [E26, Remark 8.1] state that it is exactly $1$.
- Campana, Demailly and Peternell state that a compact complex threefold homeomorphic to $S^6$ has algebraic dimension $0$ ([CDP98, Corollary 4.2]; [CDP20, Corollary 2.3]). This statement is false. Every member of the family is a counterexample.
- [CDP20, Theorem 2.2] is false. Every member of the family satisfies its hypotheses and violates each of its three conclusions.
- The printed proof uses an inference at singular fibres that does not follow [CDP20, p. 692]. An explicit test family shows this independently of the $S^6$ construction [N].
- Earlier text in this repository said that the package does not establish a CDP20 counterexample. That caution was misplaced, and the text has been corrected.

## 1. The family

**[E26, Theorem 4.1].**

- Choose local monodromy invariants $a_0\in\tfrac13\Lambda^{T_0}$ and $a_1\in\tfrac14\Lambda^{T_1}$ with $|12\,\psi(a_0+a_1)|=1$, for example $a_0=\tfrac13e_4$, $a_1=-\tfrac14f_4$.
- Then for every $u$ in a sufficiently small punctured disc, the compact complex threefold $Y=X(u,a_0,a_1)$ is diffeomorphic to $S^6$.

The proof has four steps:

1. $Y$ is simply connected (van Kampen).
2. $Y$ has the integral homology of $S^6$ (Leray spectral sequence).
3. By the Hurewicz theorem, $Y$ is a homotopy $6$-sphere. Smale's generalized Poincaré theorem makes it homeomorphic to $S^6$.
4. By the $h$-cobordism theorem and $\Theta_6=0$ (Kervaire–Milnor), $Y$ is diffeomorphic to $S^6$.

**Engel's construction.**

1. Start from the rational elliptic surface with singular fibres of Kodaira types $IV^*$, $III$, $I_1$.
2. Take the principal $\mathbb C^*$-bundle of $\mathcal O(P-O)$, where $P$ generates the Mordell–Weil group.
3. Take the quotient by a lift of translation by $6P$.
4. Fill in the fibre over $\infty$ by a Mumford construction.
5. Apply logarithmic transforms of multiplicities $3$ and $4$ over $0$ and $1$.

[AC26] reaches the same family through the period matrix $\Pi(z)$ of its §3. [E26, §1.3] gives the dictionary between the two descriptions.

**The fibration.** Each member $X$ has a holomorphic surjection $f\colon X\to\mathbb P^1$, with these fibres:

- complex $2$-tori away from $0,1,\infty$;
- multiple fibres of multiplicities $3$ and $4$ over $0$ and $1$, with bielliptic reductions;
- over $\infty$, a fibre $W$ with normal crossings that is not normal.

$W$ is the image of the degree-six del Pezzo surface $dP_6$, with the six sides of its boundary hexagon glued in three opposite pairs [AC26, Main Theorem (2)].

**How the family varies.** The complex structure genuinely varies with $u$, and the total space over the punctured disc is a smooth $S^6$-bundle [E26, Remark 8.1].

**Invariants of each member.**

- $H_1(X;\mathbb Z)=H_2(X;\mathbb Z)=0$.
- $c_3(X)=e(S^6)=2$.
- $a(X)=1$.
- Every meromorphic function on $X$ is pulled back from $\mathbb P^1$ along $f$ [AC26, Main Theorem (5)].

## 2. The false statements

**[CDP98].**

- Claim: a compact complex threefold $X$ with $b_2(X)=0$ and $a(X)>0$ has $c_3(X)=0$.
- Deduction: a compact complex threefold homeomorphic to $S^6$ has $a(X)=0$ ([CDP98, Corollary 4.2], the locator cited in [E26, §1.1]).
- The proof rests on [CDP98, Lemma 1.5]. The authors' corrigendum [CDP20] states that this lemma is incorrect as stated and re-proves the results in two cases.

**[CDP20, Theorem 2.2], as stated in [AC26, §1.2].**

- Hypotheses: $H_1(X,\mathbb Z)=H_2(X,\mathbb Z)=0$, $a(X)=1$, and the algebraic reduction $f\colon X\to C$ is holomorphic.
- Conclusions:
  - (a) $H^i(X,T_X\otimes M)=0$ for $i\neq1$ and generic $M\in\operatorname{Pic}^0(X)$;
  - (b) $\chi(X,T_X\otimes M)\le0$;
  - (c) $c_3(X)\le0$.

**[CDP20, Corollary 2.3].** If $X$ is homeomorphic to $S^6$, then $a(X)=0$.

**Why every member of the family is a counterexample.** Each member $X$ satisfies the hypotheses of Theorem 2.2, with $C=\mathbb P^1$. It violates each conclusion:

- (c) fails, because $c_3(X)=2$.
- (b) fails, for every $M\in\operatorname{Pic}(X)$. Since $H^2(X;\mathbb Q)=H^4(X;\mathbb Q)=0$, the classes $c_1(X)$, $c_2(X)$ and $c_1(M)$ vanish rationally. Hirzebruch–Riemann–Roch then gives $\chi(X,T_X\otimes M)=\tfrac12c_3(X)=1$.
- (a) fails. For any $M$, the vanishing in (a) would give $\chi(X,T_X\otimes M)=-h^1(X,T_X\otimes M)\le0$, contradicting $\chi(X,T_X\otimes M)=1$.

So Theorem 2.2 and Corollary 2.3 of [CDP20] are false. So is the claim of [CDP98], including [CDP98, Corollary 4.2].

**Related statements that fail for $X$** [AC26, §§1.2, 10]: [CDP20, Lemma 4.2], [CDP20, Corollary 5.2] and [CDP20, Proposition 5.5] (that is, [CDP98, Theorem 3.1, Remark 3.8(1)]). Their printed proofs presuppose trivial monodromy.

**What $X$ does not contradict.** $X$ does not contradict [CDP20, Theorem 2.1] or [LRS]. Their hypothesis is a non-constant meromorphic map $X\dashrightarrow\mathbb P^1$ that is not holomorphic. Every meromorphic function on $X$ is pulled back from $\mathbb P^1$, so $X$ has no such map.

## 3. The inference that does not follow

**The printed step.**

- The proof of [CDP20, Theorem 2.2] invokes [CDP20, Proposition 2.4] under the hypothesis $R^2f_*(T_X\otimes L)=0$.
- It obtains this vanishing fibre by fibre, with the twist $A=(L^\vee\otimes K_X)|_S$ on each fibre $S$.
- For an irreducible reduced fibre $S$, it deduces $H^0(S,\Omega^1_X|_S\otimes A)=0$ from the conormal sequence of $S$ and the vanishing of $H^0(S,\tilde\Omega^1_S\otimes A)$, where $\tilde\Omega^1_S=\Omega^1_S/\text{torsion}$ [CDP20, p. 692].

**Why it does not follow.** The deduction is valid when $S$ is normal. It is not valid in general. The conormal sequence involves $\Omega^1_S$, not its torsion-free quotient $\tilde\Omega^1_S$:

- The torsion of $\Omega^1_S$ is present exactly at the non-normal points of $S$ [R, Proposition 5.3].
- At a reduced fibre with normal crossings, the deduction needs the extra hypothesis $H^0(\tilde S,\eta^*A)=0$, where $\eta\colon\tilde S\to S$ is the normalization [R, Proposition 5.5].

**Two independent examples of the failure.**

1. **A test family** [N, Theorem 4.4, Remark 4.5]. A proper one-parameter family over a disc, with smooth total space and central fibre (nodal cubic)$\times\mathbb P^1$:
   - A non-torsion line bundle on that fibre makes both endpoint groups of the displayed sequence vanish.
   - The ambient twisted cotangent group is nonzero.
   - This family does not satisfy the global hypotheses of Theorem 2.2. It shows that the inference at p. 692 is not valid.
2. **The cusp fibre $W$ of the $S^6$ family** [AC26, §10.2, Lemma 10.3, Theorem 10.5]:
   - The ambient differential $df$ restricts to $W$ with coefficients vanishing on the double curve.
   - It gives a nonzero section of $\Omega^1_X|_W\otimes A$ that vanishes in $\tilde\Omega^1_W\otimes A$.
   - Consequently $R^2f_*(T_X\otimes L)\neq0$ for every holomorphic line bundle $L$ on $X$, and hypothesis (1) of [CDP20, Proposition 2.4] holds for no $L$.
   - [R, Proposition 5.5(b)] needs two inputs: $\eta^*A\cong\mathcal O$ [AC26, Theorem 10.5, Step 1], and $H^0(W,\tilde\Omega^1_W\otimes A)=0$ [AC26, remark on CDP20 after its Main Theorem]. With them, the extra hypothesis fails because $H^0(dP_6,\mathcal O)=\mathbb C$, and the failure at $W$ has dimension exactly one: $H^0(W,\Omega^1_X|_W\otimes A)\cong\mathbb C$.

**What survives** [N, Theorem 5.2 and Corollary 5.3]. If one line bundle $L_0$ has $H^2(X,T_X\otimes L_0)=0$, then the generic vanishing and $c_3(X)\le0$ follow. On every member of the family this hypothesis fails for every $L_0$, since $c_3=2$.

## 4. Earlier wording in this repository

Until this note, [s6/README.md](README.md), [s6/ATTEMPTS.md](ATTEMPTS.md) and the root [README.md](../README.md) said that the package does not establish a CDP20 counterexample, or does not certify the global steps of the $S^6$ construction.

That caution was misplaced:

- Theorem 2.2 and Corollary 2.3 of [CDP20] are false, and so is [CDP98, Corollary 4.2].
- The family above is a counterexample.
- The construction has two independent proofs.

The S6 record's paper [R] and its correction note [N] also left the counterexample open. So did the dated files in this folder:

- the 6 September key-advances reader and its TeX source;
- the 9 September frozen-project guide.

These files keep their original wording as records of their dates. This note supersedes their status statements.
