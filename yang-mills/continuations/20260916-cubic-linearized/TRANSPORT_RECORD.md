# Publication transport record

The mathematical source and checker identities were checked before publication.
A first checker text upload had one coefficient transcription mismatch in the
independent fourth-energy regression: its unmultiplied adjacent-pair denominator
was typed as 1053 instead of the locally tested 117. The blob identity gate caught
it. The whole uploaded text was recovered in three line ranges, reconstructed
locally, and the precise difference was computed. The corrected upload has the
original tested blob `8d902750cbf5661467f00c1541ba9a77cea8ce84`.

The failed transport blob and its unattached inspection commit are excluded from
this research branch's ancestry and tree. A discarded incomplete compressed-upload
attempt is likewise excluded. No mathematical source or local receipt was changed
to accept either transport. The final full mathematical record is reconstructed
by the unchanged producer and required to have its original SHA-256.

Local direct-network fetching failed at DNS resolution, and the alternate download
service did not accept the new Git object locator. Authenticated connector reads
and content-addressed writes supplied the successful route. These were publication
transport issues, not mathematical or executed-check failures.
