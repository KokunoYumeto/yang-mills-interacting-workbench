## Independent reasoning on the displayed identities

1. For real sources, let `A=K-S-e`, `Au=0`, and `phi4=u-w`. Self-adjointness gives `<phi4,A phi4>=<w,A w>`, proving E3 with the original denominator. Graph-norm `w(tx)=O(t^5)` makes the right-hand numerator `O(t^10)`, while the denominator tends to one. Hence all coefficients through degree nine agree along every real direction. The note correctly avoids using the conjugate-linear quotient as a holomorphic function of complex parameters.
2. Collecting degree eight in the finite quotient yields E5–E6, with the nonzero denominator corrections `e2 D6+e4 D4+e6 D2`. Pairing `Ku4=S u3+e2 u2+e4` with mean-zero `u4` cancels exactly the two `e2<u2,u4>` terms and yields E9. No missing scalar or denominator term was found in this algebra.
3. Independent link-center flips force odd-multiplicity faces to form a cubical two-cycle. A nonempty finite cube filling with at least two cubes has at least ten boundary faces, from its three coordinate projections. Degree eight therefore leaves all-even multiplicities or one six-face cube boundary plus two equal face occurrences. Connectedness excludes a nonadjacent external doubled face. The 24 external faces are the two external faces at each of the cube's 12 edges; each shares one cube edge. They form one signed-coordinate orbit, as do the six marked boundary faces. Thus the structural reduction to the claimed 78 predecessor classes plus two cube classes is sound, conditional on the predecessor enumeration.
4. A minor exposition repair would strengthen E3's filling argument: finite support of marked horizontal faces alone does not guarantee that the positive-ray filling vanishes far below them. The two-cycle equations force total horizontal column parity to be constant in transverse directions, and finite support makes that constant zero. The predecessor `SIXTH_ENERGY.md` E3 supplies precisely this missing intermediate sentence. This is an abbreviated step, not a detected counterexample.
5. For a fixed oriented translated shape of widths `w_i`, the count `product_i(m+1-w_i)` follows directly from allowed minimum vertices. The multiset convention correctly introduces no factorial. This validates the counting formula, but not the missing actual 80-row width catalogue or its aggregate polynomial.
6. S33–S36 give consistent termwise bounds: the single differentiated trace has three-component norm at most one; each undifferentiated trace is bounded by two; each ordered generator has matrix norm `1/2`. Consequently `D_(nu,e)/C_(nu,e)<=3 ell/2<=36` when `ell<=24`. This checks the argument's factor, not the unavailable total budgets `C6,D6`.

No fatal mathematical discrepancy was found in this bounded inspection. The unresolved central issue is evidence availability for the large new spatial calculations.
