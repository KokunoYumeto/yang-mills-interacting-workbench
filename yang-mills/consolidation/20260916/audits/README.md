These reports were written by separate agents from the raw proof sources during the consolidation. They include fresh derivations and exact source locators. They are independent passes within the same AI-assisted workflow, not external peer review or Lean certification. Their verdicts apply to the selected claims specified in each report, not every sentence in the source session.

- [Early and middle maps and estimates](early_mid.md): spectral reconstruction, escaping-state quotient, vacuum-weighted refinement, response and local-loop estimates.
- [Gauge-native physical gap and spatial return](gauge_native.md): the coefficient-to-physical-eigenfunction argument and the fixed-spacing volume limit, including its endpoint.
- [Local conditional fibres](local_fibres.md): actual conditional variance, restricted-form inverse, retained-exterior maps and their domain qualifications.
- [Cubic and linearized finite-box return](cubic_linearized.md): channel derivations, local incidence counts, endpoint source convergence, physical spectral return and energy coefficient.
- [Exact independent cubic calculations](cubic_calculations.py) and [saved output](cubic_calculations.json): a separate implementation of the stated rational and local-geometry checks, importing no delivered checker code.
- [What each supplied checker actually tests](checker_scope.md): finite ranges, arithmetic, fixtures, sampling and platform serialization limits.

The original proof sources and their pinned receipt inputs remain unchanged. Reader-only typography corrections and mathematical domain clarifications are visible in the reports and the reader errata. [VALIDATION.md](../VALIDATION.md) records fresh execution separately.
